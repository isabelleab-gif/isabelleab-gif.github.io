Peer Assistance for Learning Application Flyer

Every year, the Peer Assistance for Learning program requires an updated application flyer, as we try to build stronger pools of applicants. In my first year as a designer for the PAL program, I helped implement and advertise a flyer that brought our applicant pool from 17 to 96 in one academic year!

Community Resiliency Model Bike Flyer Redesign

This is a design I'm quite proud of. The Community Resiliency Model is a stress response model that has recently been instituted at Virginia Tech by counselors and wellness groups, with the goal of helping students regulate their nervouse systems. As part of the Cook Counseling Center's outreach program, us PALs take a motorized bike filled with fidget toys to a busy spot on campus. We try to reach as many students as possible, offering free fidget toys and talking to them about the Counseling Center services available to them. This poster was designed to accompany the bike, meant to catch the eyes of more students.

Stress Ball Workshop Promotional Flyer

This is my most recent design. While it was a last minute project (compared to others I've been assigned), this is one where I got to play around with colors outside of VT's brand book. Again, my focus was catching the eyes of students who might be interested in taking a hands-on approach to stress relief by joining a stress-ball-making workshop.
